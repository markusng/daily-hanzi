Monday to Friday each bring three new words: a noun, a verb, and another kind of word (adjective, adverb, measure word, pronoun, phrase and so on). Saturday lists the week’s 15 words for review; Sunday turns them into a self-test.
Each 2027 month teaches its theme’s most commonly used words. November and December 2026 are a warm-up with the next most common words from every theme, and January to March 2028 finish with the less common ones. When a festival falls on a weekend, its words come on the nearest weekday.
Cantonese uses traditional characters in Hong Kong and Macau. Where everyday spoken Cantonese prefers a different word, it is shown under “In spoken Cantonese”. Jyutping tones: 1 high · 2 high rising · 3 mid · 4 low falling · 5 low rising · 6 low.
